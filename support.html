Common questions

How do I start a guided journey?

Home → + → New Guided Journey. Choose a start point and StrideMate plans a route with an audio story at each stop. Stories play as you arrive, even with your screen locked.

How do race cheers work?

Create one with Home → + → New Race Cheer, record a message for points along the course, and share the code. The runner enters it under Home → Cheers → "Have a cheer code?" and hears each message as they reach that point.

Stories or cheers aren't playing.

Check that location access is set to "While Using" or "Always" in Settings → StrideMate → Location, and that your ringer volume is up. Audio plays only during an active session.

How do I manage or cancel StrideMate+?

In the iPhone Settings app: tap your name → Subscriptions → StrideMate. To restore a purchase on a new phone: You → Settings (gear) → Membership → Restore purchases.

How do I delete my account?

You → Settings (gear) → Delete Account. This removes your profile, journeys and cheers.
