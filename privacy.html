Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

The short version.

We collect what StrideMate needs to work: your account, your activities, the journeys and cheers you create, and your location while you're using a journey or tracking an activity.

Photos and videos you capture on a walk are kept privately in your account, so your journal is on every device you sign in to (and, if you allow it, they're also saved to your photo library). Cheer recordings are uploaded so the runner can hear them.

We use AI services to write and narrate your tours, and we ask for your permission before sending them anything. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use advertising trackers.

You can delete your account, and its data, from the app at any time.

This policy explains how 1001737122 Ontario Inc. ("we", "us") handles personal information in the StrideMate app ("StrideMate" or "the app"). If you have questions, contact us at shakiba.rahnama97@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you sign up we collect your email address and the name you choose. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the account identifier and the email address that service shares with us (for Apple, this can be a private relay address). We also store your preferences: activity type, interests, guide voice, and units.

During setup we ask what you're hoping to use StrideMate for (for example, discovering new places or cheering on a runner). We keep the answer you first give, alongside how you go on to use the app, so we can understand what people come for and improve StrideMate. You can change your answer in Profile; your first answer is deleted with your account.

Your public profile

You choose a unique handle and a display name, and you can add a profile photo, a short bio, and a location. These, along with the month you joined, make up your public profile: anyone signed in to StrideMate can find you by your handle and see it. Only add what you're happy for others to see. Before we save a profile photo or profile text, we check it with OpenAI's moderation service to keep out content that isn't allowed (see section 3). If you're a StrideMate+ member, your profile shows a member badge, and founding members a crown.

Your profile can also show your activity: your stats (such as journeys, landmarks visited, cities toured, distance, and estimated steps), your weekly streak, milestones, a 12-week activity chart, and your journal. Journal entries show their name, date, type, distance, city, a public note you can add, and their photos. Your private reflections, videos, and routes are never shown. These are shown to everyone by default. In Profile → Sharing you can choose who sees each one (everyone, your crew, or only you), make your whole profile private, and hide single journal entries. A private profile shows only your photo, name, handle, bio, location, member since, and badge. To show your stats, the app saves a summary of your totals to your account. Before anyone else sees a journal photo, we check it with OpenAI's moderation service.

Your crew

You can ask people to join your crew, and accept or decline their requests. We store who is in your crew and the requests between you. Crew members can see what you've set to "crew" in Sharing. Declining a request or removing someone isn't shown to them. If someone you declined asks again within 30 days, their request is held and not shown to you. Blocking someone removes them from your crew.

Sending to your crew

You can send a recommendation or a saved landmark to someone in your crew, with a note. We store what you sent, who you sent it to, your note, where the recommendation originally came from (including if it was passed on to you), and whether they added it to their Library. They see your name and note. Before a note is sent, we check it with OpenAI's moderation service. If you delete your account, what you've sent stays with the people you sent it to, with your name.

Reports and blocks

If you report a profile, we keep the report: the reason, any note you add, and a copy of the profile as it was (including its photo), so we can review it. The person isn't told who reported them. If you block someone, we keep that so you're hidden from each other. Reports may be kept after either account is deleted, for as long as needed to deal with them and to keep people using StrideMate safe.

Birthday and gender

When you sign up we ask for your date of birth, to make sure StrideMate is right for your age (see section 9). If you're under the minimum age, we don't keep the date you entered and we close the account you just made. You can also tell us your gender (woman, man, non-binary, your own description, or prefer not to say). This is optional.

Apart from the age check, we use your birthday and gender to understand who uses StrideMate and which recommendations people pass on to each other. This can include looking at a person's age range and gender alongside what they send, receive, and save. They're never shown on your profile, shared with other users, or sent to AI services. Anything you write to describe your gender stays private to your account and is never used in this analysis. You can change your birthday, or change or remove your gender, in Profile → About you.

Location

StrideMate uses your device's precise location to:

  • track distance, time, and pace during an activity;
  • play each story or cheer when you reach the right place or distance, including while your screen is locked; and
  • plan a guided journey around your starting point.

We use location in the background only while an activity is in progress. We do not save your continuous location trail to our servers. We do store a journey's starting point, its planned route, and the location of landmarks you bookmark.

Activities and journeys

For each activity you save we store its name, type, distance, duration, start and end time, how many landmarks you visited, the city it started in, and technical GPS-quality statistics (such as signal accuracy and gaps) that help us fix tracking problems. For guided journeys we store the route, the landmarks, the generated narration text and audio, your recap, your written reflection, ratings, feedback, and the recommendations made for you (podcast episodes, videos, films and series, songs, and books), along with their status and whether you opened them.

Race cheers

When you create a cheer we store its details (name, distance, race date, location, checkpoint notes) and the audio messages you record. When you share a cheer, the people who redeem your code can see the cheer and play its recordings. See section 5.

Photos and videos

Photos and videos you take in the app are stored on your device and uploaded to your account, so your journal is backed up and appears on any device you sign in to. We keep a smaller copy (photos are resized and videos compressed), stored privately so only you can access it, and we don't use it for anything else. Videos upload only on Wi-Fi or another connection that isn't metered. Deleting a photo or video in the app deletes it from your account too.

If you allow it, StrideMate also saves a copy to your device's photo library. StrideMate asks only for permission to add photos, so it can't see anything else in your library. You can turn this off in Profile → Preferences.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple. We never see your card or payment details. We receive the transaction information Apple provides (such as the product, date, and a transaction identifier) and link it to your account so we can apply your subscription or credits. We keep a log of each purchase check (the transaction identifier, product, and result) so we can resolve any problem with a purchase.

Push notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a push token for your device, which Apple's push service uses to deliver notifications from us (for example, a crew request). Signing out removes your device's token from your account. You can turn crew notifications off in Profile → Notifications.

Crash and diagnostic reports

If StrideMate crashes, freezes, or something important fails (for example a journey that can't be created or a purchase error), the app sends us a technical report: what went wrong, the app and iOS version, and your device model. Crash reports come from Apple's built-in diagnostics and contain technical information about the app's code, not your content or location. Reports are linked to your account so we can help if you contact us, are visible only to us, and are deleted automatically after 90 days.

Microphone and camera

We access the microphone only when you record a cheer, and the camera only when you choose to take a photo or video.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide StrideMate: create journeys, narrate them, track activities, deliver cheers, and show your stats and journal.
  • To personalise journeys to your interests, your food and drink preferences, the time of day, and the weather where you are.
  • To manage your subscription, credits, and purchases, and to prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To send notifications you allow, such as crew requests, your monthly journeys, streak reminders, or reminders about things you saved.
  • To respond to you when you contact us, and to improve the app using feedback you give us and which recommendations people open.
  • To find and fix crashes and errors.
  • To keep StrideMate safe: checking profile photos and text, reviewing reports, and applying blocks.
  • To check that StrideMate is right for your age, and to understand who uses it and how recommendations spread, using age range and gender.
  • To keep the service fair and affordable, by counting how often each account uses our AI and mapping services and applying daily limits.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles.

4. Where your information is stored

Our servers are operated by Supabase in the United States. Our other providers may process data in the United States or other countries. Where the law requires, we rely on appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

5. Sharing a cheer

Each cheer has a share code. Anyone you give the code to can add the cheer to their account, see its details, and play its recordings, including recordings you add later. Only share codes with people you trust, and only record other people's voices with their permission. You can delete a cheer at any time, which removes it for everyone it was shared with.

Recordings are stored at web addresses that are hard to guess. Anyone who obtains a recording's address could play it, so don't record anything you'd consider sensitive.

6. How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account in the app, we immediately delete your account and the data linked to it, including your cheer recordings, journey audio, and journal photos and videos, and, if you signed in with Apple, we tell Apple to disconnect StrideMate from your Apple ID. The app also erases the data it stored on your phone. Copies may remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days before they're overwritten. We keep the purchase-check log described above where the law requires us to keep records of transactions.

Photos already saved to your photo library are yours and stay there. When you delete your account, the app also removes that account's photos, videos, and other data from the device you delete it on; deleting the app removes everything it stored.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Permissions. You can turn location, microphone, camera, and notification access on or off at any time in your device's Settings.
  • Birthday and gender. You can change your birthday, or change or remove your gender, in Profile → About you.
  • AI permission. You can withdraw your permission for AI processing in Profile → Privacy.
  • Edit or delete. You can edit or delete journeys, cheers, reflections, and saved items in the app.
  • Delete your account. Go to You → Profile → Delete Account.
  • Access and portability. You can ask us for a copy of your data.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, such as to object to or restrict certain processing, or to complain to your local data protection authority (in Canada, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada). You can exercise your rights by emailing shakiba.rahnama97@gmail.com. We'll respond within the time the law requires and won't treat you differently for exercising your rights.

Where the GDPR applies, we process your information to provide the service you asked for (contract), to keep StrideMate secure and improve it, including checking profile content and handling reports (legitimate interests), and, for location, microphone, camera, notifications, photo library access, and sending data to AI providers, with your consent, which you can withdraw in Settings or in the app. We use your date of birth to check your age (legal obligation and contract) and to understand who uses StrideMate and how recommendations spread (legitimate interests). If you choose to tell us your gender, we process it with your consent, which you can withdraw by removing it in Profile → About you.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and access rules limit each account to its own data and to cheers shared with it. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

9. Children

StrideMate is for people 13 and older, or 16 and older in the European Union and European Economic Area. We ask for your date of birth when you sign up. If you're under the minimum age, we don't keep it and we close the account straight away. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the date above and let you know in the app before they take effect.
